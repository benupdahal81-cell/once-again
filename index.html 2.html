NEPAL • MOUNTAIN DESTINATION

Mustang

Mustang is one of Nepal's most fascinating travel destinations, known for its dramatic Himalayan landscapes, ancient villages, monasteries and unique Tibetan-influenced culture.

A Mustang journey can include beautiful places such as Jomsom, Marpha, Kagbeni and Muktinath while experiencing the remarkable landscapes of the Himalayas.

NEPAL • LAKES & MOUNTAINS

Pokhara

Pokhara is a perfect destination for travelers looking for beautiful lakes, mountain views, peaceful surroundings and exciting activities.

Visitors can enjoy Phewa Lake, Sarangkot, Davis Falls, caves, mountain views and a wide range of comfortable hotels and resorts.
